# CryptoTrack Research Plan

## Working title
CryptoTrack: Probabilistic Multi-Source Cryptocurrency Prediction and Cost-Aware Portfolio Optimization

## Research question
Does replacing binary movement signals with probabilistic forecasts, while using leakage-resistant walk-forward validation and transaction-cost-aware portfolio construction, improve out-of-sample risk-adjusted portfolio performance?

## Main hypotheses
H1: Multi-source features add predictive information beyond market-only features.
H2: Probabilistic forecasts are more useful for allocation than hard binary signals.
H3: Transaction costs and turnover materially affect gross-to-net performance.
H4: Walk-forward evaluation provides a more conservative out-of-sample estimate than test-set-driven tuning.

## Experiments
E1 Market only
E2 Market + sentiment
E3 Market + Google Trends
E4 Market + sentiment + Trends
E5 Binary vs probabilistic SVM
E6 Benchmark portfolios vs probability-aware portfolio
E7 Transaction-cost sensitivity
E8 Robustness across horizons and periods

## Prediction metrics
Accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, Brier score.

## Portfolio metrics
Return, volatility, Sharpe, Sortino, maximum drawdown, turnover, transaction costs, net return.

## Integrity rule
No numerical result is written into the paper until generated by the implementation and tied to a documented experiment configuration.
